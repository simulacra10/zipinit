Encrypted Email (PGP)

We publish a public key so anyone can send us encrypted email. If you use Proton Mail, this happens automatically: Proton looks up our key over Web Key Directory (WKD) and encrypts the message before it leaves your outbox. You don’t have to do anything.

If you don’t have a Proton Mail account yet, Proton Mail’s free tier takes a couple of minutes to set up, and it encrypts your outgoing mail automatically whenever the recipient supports it — including us.

If you use a different provider, you can still send us encrypted mail. Most PGP-aware clients (Thunderbird, Mailvelope, GPG Suite) can also fetch our key automatically via WKD, or you can import it manually below.
