* Purpose

This file tracks the work needed to strengthen ZIPINIT's explanation of ZIP apportionment, resolve inconsistencies across the site, and directly answer the four strongest objections:

1. Politicization or manipulation of ZIP geography
2. Population equality and one person, one vote
3. Legislature size, cost, and practicality
4. Discretion in subdividing and combining ZIPs

* Site-Wide Language Guide

** Avoid

- "One representative per ZIP"
- "Representation by ZIP rather than population"
- "ZIPs cannot be gerrymandered"
- "Population variance is a feature, not a defect"
- "ZIP boundaries are fixed"
- "The USPS has no influence over the system"

** Prefer

- "ZIP geography is the starting point for representation."
- "Population equality remains a binding design constraint."
- "Large and small ZIP exceptions follow predetermined rules."
- "ZIP apportionment sharply limits discretionary line-drawing."
- "The controlling geography is legally frozen for each apportionment cycle."
- "Administrators execute the rule rather than design the constituency."
- "The proposal narrows and exposes opportunities for manipulation rather than claiming to eliminate every possible abuse."
