The fastest way to reach us is Signal. For anything you want kept private
in transit, use encrypted email instead.

## Signal

Contact us on [Signal](https://signal.org/) @zipinit.12

## Email

General contact: [expand@zipinit.org](mailto:expand@zipinit.org)

## Encrypted Email (PGP)

We publish a public key so anyone can send us encrypted email. If you use
[Proton Mail](https://proton.me/mail), this happens
automatically: Proton looks up our key over Web Key Directory (WKD) and
encrypts the message before it leaves your outbox. You don’t have to
do anything.

If you don’t have a Proton Mail account yet, [Proton
Mail’s free tier](https://proton.me/mail) takes a couple of minutes to set up, and it
encrypts your outgoing mail automatically whenever the recipient supports
it — including us.

If you use a different provider, you can still send us encrypted mail.
Most PGP-aware clients (Thunderbird, Mailvelope, GPG Suite) can also fetch
our key automatically via WKD, or you can import it manually below.
