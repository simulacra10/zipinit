The Initiative posts and discusses ZIP apportionment on
[Nostr](https://nostr.com/), an open protocol with no central server and no
platform that can silence an account. Posts from the official account
(`zip@zipinit.org`) are shown below, pulled live from the network — this
page has no database of its own.

Individual Journal articles and policy papers also carry their own comment
sections, anchored to that page's URL, using the same underlying protocol.

Posting from this page isn't available yet. To reply now, follow the
account from any Nostr client.
