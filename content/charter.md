## <span class="num">I.</span> Purpose {#purpose}

The ZIP Apportionment Initiative exists for one purpose only:

**To articulate, defend, and advance the adoption of ZIP-code–based apportionment
for state legislative representation, beginning with Maryland.**

This Initiative is not a general advocacy organization, political party,
or permanent institution. It exists solely to pursue clearly defined
institutional outcomes related to this objective.

## <span class="num">II.</span> Scope of Work {#scope}

The Initiative may engage only in activities directly related to its stated
purpose, including:

- Research, writing, and publication of essays and briefs
- Public argument, debate, and critique
- Communication with legislators, officials, and the public
- Formal testimony or submissions when appropriate
- Open preservation of arguments and source materials

The Initiative shall not expand its mission beyond this scope.

## <span class="num">III.</span> Volunteer Structure {#volunteer}

The Initiative is entirely volunteer-driven.

This initiative was started by one person who thinks representation should be
rooted in place, not political convenience. It's volunteer-run because that's
what keeps it honest &mdash; no funders, no board, nothing to protect.

- No salaries, stipends, or compensation of any kind
- Participation is voluntary and non-exclusive
- Roles are functional, temporary, and task-specific
- No permanent titles or spokesperson positions

Reimbursement for direct, documented expenses may be permitted on a
pass-through basis only.

## <span class="num">IV.</span> Financial Constraints {#financial}

The Initiative shall not exist to raise or manage funds as an end in itself.

- No paid staff
- No ongoing financial obligations
- Funds used only for direct, mission-specific expenses

## <span class="num">V.</span> Transparency and Open Publication {#transparency}

All substantive work produced by the Initiative shall be made publicly available.

The Initiative claims no ownership over the ideas it advances. Its goal is
dissemination, not control.

## <span class="num">VI.</span> Outcome-Based Termination {#termination}

The Initiative has no fixed lifespan. It exists only until one of the following
conditions is met:

- ZIP-based apportionment is formally adopted
- A ZIP-based proposal is formally introduced into legislative proceedings
- A governmental body issues a definitive ruling on the proposal
- The originators publicly declare the effort concluded or abandoned

Upon the occurrence of any such condition, the Initiative shall dissolve
automatically.

## <span class="num">VII.</span> Non-Institutional Principle {#non-institutional}

The Initiative explicitly rejects institutional permanence.

It shall not seek to grow, brand itself, or persist beyond its stated purpose.

## <span class="num">VIII.</span> Amendment {#amendment}

This Charter may be amended only to clarify scope, tighten constraints,
or accelerate termination. No amendment may expand the mission or
permit compensation.

## <span class="num">IX.</span> Closing Statement {#closing}

*This Initiative exists to force a serious institutional question into public
consideration. It does not exist to survive. When its work is complete—or
rendered impossible—it will end.*
